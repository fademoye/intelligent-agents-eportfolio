## Summary of Development

The strongest areas of development were:

- critical thinking and analysis;
- IT and digital skills;
- problem-solving;
- ethical awareness;
- resilience;
- research skills.

The module also showed that I need to improve:

- early project planning;
- formal documentation of team responsibilities;
- continuous reflection;
- advanced deployment skills;
- evaluation of output quality;
- the use of academic evidence in technical decision-making.

## Overall Reflection

The skills matrix demonstrates that my development during the module was not limited to technical programming.

I also developed greater awareness of teamwork, professional responsibility, ethical design, critical evaluation and the importance of realistic project scope.
