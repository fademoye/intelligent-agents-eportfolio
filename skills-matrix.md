# Professional Skills Matrix

This skills matrix evaluates my professional development during the Intelligent Agents module.

The ratings used are:

- Developing
- Competent
- Strong

| Skill | Starting Level | Current Level | Evidence from the Module | Further Development Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Time Management | Developing | Competent | I completed forum activities, team meetings, group work, individual implementation, testing and presentation preparation, but some documentation and portfolio tasks were concentrated close to the deadline. | I need to begin major tasks earlier, set internal deadlines and complete evidence collection throughout the module. |
| Commercial Awareness | Developing | Competent | The ResearchMate project required me to consider a real user problem, potential users, practical value and responsible deployment. | I need to develop stronger skills in market validation, product feasibility, user research and competitor analysis. |
| Critical Thinking and Analysis | Competent | Strong | I compared agent architectures, evaluated differences between the Week 7 design and Week 11 implementation, and analysed technical and ethical limitations. | I need to strengthen the use of academic evidence when justifying technical decisions. |
| Communication and Literacy | Competent | Strong | I contributed to collaborative discussions, group meetings, project documentation and presentation material. | I need to make technical explanations more concise and adapt them more consistently for different audiences. |
| IT and Digital Skills | Competent | Strong | I used Python, GitHub, GitHub Pages, pytest, modular software design and technical documentation. | I need to improve my knowledge of deployment, APIs, vector databases and advanced agent orchestration. |
| Resilience | Competent | Strong | I continued developing the project despite technical constraints, changes in scope and time pressure. | I need to respond to setbacks earlier by revising plans and seeking feedback sooner. |
| Interpersonal Skills | Competent | Competent | I participated in virtual teamwork, shared discussions and group decision-making. | I need to become more confident in clarifying expectations and challenging unclear responsibilities. |
| Problem-Solving | Competent | Strong | I simplified the original design into a realistic prototype, resolved technical problems and used testing to validate the workflow. | I need to document problems and solutions more consistently during development. |
| Ethical Awareness | Developing | Strong | I examined bias, explainability, source reliability, privacy, student overreliance and responsible deployment. | I need to translate ethical concerns into measurable technical safeguards and testing criteria. |
| Teamwork and Leadership | Competent | Competent | I contributed to the ResearchMate team project, attended meetings and supported shared project decisions. | I need to use clearer role allocation, action tracking and responsibility matrices in future group work. |
| Critical Reflection | Developing | Competent | I evaluated my technical decisions, emotional responses, teamwork experience, mistakes and future actions. | I need to maintain reflective notes throughout a project rather than mainly at the end. |
| Research Skills | Competent | Competent | I explored intelligent-agent architectures, ethical issues, technical tools and research-planning requirements. | I need to strengthen source evaluation and make greater use of primary academic literature. |

## Summary of Development

My strongest areas of development were critical thinking, IT and digital skills, problem-solving, resilience and ethical awareness.

The main areas requiring further development are early project planning, documentation of team responsibilities, continuous reflection, output-quality evaluation and stronger use of academic evidence.

## Overall Reflection

The module developed more than my technical programming ability. It also strengthened my awareness of teamwork, professional responsibility, ethical design, critical evaluation and realistic project scope.
