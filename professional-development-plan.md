## Priority Actions

My three immediate priorities are:

1. Improve project planning by setting internal deadlines and completing testing earlier.
2. Strengthen agent evaluation by testing both technical functionality and the quality of generated outputs.
3. Improve responsible AI practice by including explainability, source verification and human review in future designs.

## Longer-Term Development

In the longer term, I intend to develop a more advanced version of the academic research-planning agent.

This version would include:

- stronger agent orchestration;
- reliable academic-source retrieval;
- source verification;
- explainable recommendations;
- human-in-the-loop review;
- privacy and security controls;
- a web-based user interface;
- structured user testing.

## How Progress Will Be Reviewed

I will review this plan at the end of each future module or major project.

For each development area, I will record:

- what action was completed;
- what evidence was produced;
- what improvement occurred;
- what remains unresolved;
- whether the target date needs to be revised.

This will help ensure that the development plan remains active rather than becoming a one-time reflection.
