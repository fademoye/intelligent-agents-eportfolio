# Collaborative Discussion Summaries

I contributed to separate discussions on agent based systems and generative artificial intelligence ethics.

In the agent based systems discussion, I examined organisational benefits such as automation, fraud monitoring and compliance support. My responses to Hamad and John considered autonomy, multi agent cooperation, BDI reasoning and the importance of human oversight in regulated environments.

In the generative AI discussion, I explored bias, copyright, misinformation, deepfakes and accountability. Kanna Ghanim Saeed Yaryou Almansoori introduced model cards and pre deployment safeguards. Stuart Gibb discussed recommendation bias and the increasing difficulty of fact checking. Julio Espinosa Rifel challenged reliance on human detection and introduced C2PA provenance.

I also responded to discussions about creator exploitation, model memorisation, open models and sustainability. These contributions strengthened my understanding that responsible AI requires transparent design, testing, governance, human review and shared accountability across developers, organisations, platforms, regulators and users.
