# Team Project: ResearchMate

ResearchMate was a collaborative project to design a hybrid multi agent system for academic research automation. The proposed system used Planning, Retrieval, Processing, Ranking and Storage Agents to decompose research queries, retrieve academic sources, summarise information, assess credibility and produce structured outputs.

My agreed role was Team Lead and Project Coordinator. I organised meetings, supported task allocation, managed timelines, integrated the report, proofread the final document and supported submission management. The kick off document and team contract provide formal evidence of these responsibilities.

The project strengthened my understanding of agent coordination, virtual teamwork and project leadership. I learned that clear ownership, documented decisions and realistic scope are essential when developing complex systems in a distributed team.
